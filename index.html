English for Fashion & Clothing

Master vocabulary, grammar, and conversation skills

Key Vocabulary

Category 1: Formal & Professional

Blazer (سترة رسمية)

Def: A lightweight jacket that looks like a suit jacket but is cut more casually.

  • She wore a blue blazer to the interview.
  • A blazer can make any outfit look professional.
  • I need to get my blazer dry-cleaned.

Trousers (سراويل)

Def: An outer garment covering the body from the waist to the ankles, with a separate part for each leg.

  • He bought a pair of grey wool trousers.
  • These trousers are a bit too long for me.
  • Make sure your trousers are ironed.

Cuffs (أكمام)

Def: The end part of a sleeve, where the material is turned back or a separate band is sewn on.

  • He fastened the silver buttons on his cuffs.
  • The cuffs of his shirt were slightly frayed.
  • Fold your cuffs if you are going to wash the dishes.

Category 2: Casual & Outerwear

Hoodie (سترة بقلنسوة)

Def: A sweatshirt with a hood attached.

  • I love wearing an oversized hoodie on Sundays.
  • Put your hoodie on; it's starting to rain.
  • He pulled the drawstring on his hoodie.

Cardigan (سترة صوفية)

Def: A knitted sweater that opens at the front with buttons or a zipper.

  • My grandmother knitted this wool cardigan for me.
  • It's chilly, so I brought a light cardigan.
  • The cardigan has beautiful wooden buttons.

Outfit (ملابس/طقم)

Def: A set of clothes worn together, typically for a particular occasion.

  • That is a very stylish outfit you're wearing!
  • I need to choose an outfit for the wedding.
  • She bought a whole new outfit for her first day at work.

Common Expressions

1. To get dressed (يرتدي ملابسه)

Context: Used for the general process of putting on all your clothes.

  • Hurry up and get dressed!
  • It takes me twenty minutes to get dressed in the morning.
  • I got dressed quickly because I was late.

2. To fit like a glove (يناسب تماماً)

Context: When a piece of clothing is exactly the right size and shape.

  • I tried on the dress and it fits like a glove.
  • These jeans fit him like a glove.
  • Even though I bought it online, it fits like a glove.

3. To dress up (يتأنق)

Context: To wear formal or special clothes for an event.

  • We need to dress up for the gala tonight.
  • You don't need to dress up; it's a casual party.
  • I love any excuse to dress up in a suit.

4. To suit someone (يليق بـ)

Context: When a color or style looks good on a person.

  • That shade of green really suits you!
  • Short hair doesn't suit me at all.
  • I don't think this style suits his personality.

Real-Life Dialogues

1. At the Clothing Store

Sarah: Excuse me, do you have this blazer in a smaller size?

Clerk: Let me check the back. Is that a medium?

Sarah: Yes, but it feels a bit loose around the cuffs.

Clerk: Here is a small. Try it on in the fitting room.

Sarah: Oh, this is perfect! It fits like a glove.

Clerk: That navy blue really suits you, by the way.

2. Preparing for a Party

Mark: Are we dressing up for the dinner tonight?

Jane: Yes, the invitation said it's a formal event.

Mark: Should I wear my black trousers or jeans?

Jane: Definitely the trousers. Jeans are too casual.

Mark: Okay, I'll go get dressed now.

Jane: Good idea, we're leaving in ten minutes!

Common Mistakes

I am wearing a cloth.

Correct: I am wearing clothes.

Why: "Cloth" refers to the material/fabric. "Clothes" is the plural noun for what we wear.

He wears a blue jean.

Correct: He is wearing jeans / a pair of jeans.

Why: "Jeans" is always plural because they have two legs.

This shirt is not fitting me.

Correct: This shirt doesn't fit me.

Why: "Fit" is a stative verb; we rarely use it in the continuous (-ing) form.

The Short Story: The Unexpected Interview

Ahmed woke up late and realized he had a job interview in thirty minutes! He rushed to his closet but couldn't find his favorite blazer. He decided to wear a simple cardigan instead. When he arrived, he felt underdressed, but the manager told him, "That color really suits you!" Ahmed relaxed, and even though he wasn't dressed up in a suit, he got the job because of his skills. He learned that while an outfit is important, confidence is the best thing you can wear.

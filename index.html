English for Fashion & Clothes

Interactive Lesson Plan for Language Learners

Vocabulary

Formal Style
1. Blazer (سترة رسمية): A casual suit-style jacket.
  • He wore a blue blazer to the meeting.
  • This blazer is too large for me.
  • I love the buttons on this blazer.
  • Keep your blazer on for the photo.
2. Tailored (مُفصّل): Custom-fitted clothing.
  • His tailored suit fits perfectly.
  • I prefer tailored shirts.
  • Is that dress tailored or store-bought?
  • She went to a tailored clothing shop.

Common Expressions

"Fits like a glove": Used when something fits perfectly.
  • I tried the dress and it fits like a glove!
  • Your new shoes fit like a glove.
  • It's rare to find a hat that fits like a glove.
"Dress up": To wear formal clothes.
  • We need to dress up for the wedding.
  • Do I have to dress up tonight?
  • She loves to dress up for parties.

Dialogues

Mark: Are you dressing up for the dinner tonight?

Jane: Yes, I'm wearing my tailored dress.

Mark: Does it still fit like a glove?

Jane: It does! It's my favorite outfit.

Common Mistakes

I am wearing a blue jean.

I am wearing blue jeans.

Why: "Jeans" is always plural because there are two legs.
